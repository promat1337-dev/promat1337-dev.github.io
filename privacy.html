Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to other users.

What data is accessed

  • When the owner signs in with TikTok or Google/YouTube, Clipper receives OAuth access tokens and basic account information (such as display name) needed to publish videos.
  • Clipper uses these tokens solely to upload videos to the owner's own accounts.

Storage

Tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer. No data is sent to any server operated by Clipper, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in their TikTok or Google account settings, and delete the stored tokens from their computer.

Contact

Questions: contact the app owner through the support email listed on the app's developer page.
